Fertility evaluation starts with understanding both partners

Trying for a pregnancy can bring many questions. A fertility consultation is a chance to discuss your history, understand which assessments may be useful and agree on the next steps.

Fertility concerns can involve either or both partners, so assessment should consider both. Bring previous reports, details of any earlier treatment, a medicines list and relevant menstrual records.

At The Blessed Womb in Alpha I, Greater Noida, Dr. Jyoti Gupta provides fertility evaluation and infertility-related care. Investigations and follow-up are advised according to your circumstances; everyone does not need the same tests or treatment.
