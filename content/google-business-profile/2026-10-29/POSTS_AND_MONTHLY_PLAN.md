Irregular periods? Bring a clearer picture to your consultation

If your periods have become unpredictable or your usual pattern has changed, keeping a simple record can make your consultation more useful.

Note when each period starts, how long bleeding lasts, any bleeding between periods, and symptoms such as pain or changes in flow. Also bring a list of medicines and any previous test reports.

Irregular periods can have several causes. Symptoms alone do not establish a diagnosis, and treatment depends on the assessment.

## Publishing and follow-up

1. Open the correct current-brand profile while signed into its owner/manager
   Google account. Go to **Posts → Add post → Update**.
2. Inspect the most recent Google post. Check whether the proposed first topic
   repeats it; the website's article date does not answer this.
3. Paste the description, optionally add the appropriate image, and choose
   **Learn more** with that week's full button URL. Put the URL in the button
   field rather than adding a long tracking URL to the body.
4. Publish Week 1. For future weeks, use Google's scheduling option if it is
   available in the editor. Create four separate updates; do not set one post
   to repeat every week. This plan does not create an automatic schedule.
5. After posting, verify whether Google marks it **Live**, **Pending** or
   **Not approved**. A submitted post is not necessarily public. Record its
   actual date, public link and status in `posts.json`.
6. Check website visits attributed to this campaign and `week_01`–`week_04`,
   alongside actual enquiries. Use only aggregate analytics; do not add names,
   phone numbers or medical information to tracking parameters.
7. At the end of the month, compare topics by engaged visits and enquiries
   rather than claiming that weekly posting alone caused a ranking change.

The copy uses no promotional phone number in the description; the profile's
existing phone action remains available. It avoids superlatives, guaranteed
medical outcomes, invented prices and unverified hospital/procedure claims.
No claim is made that Dr. Gupta has medically reviewed these newly drafted posts.
