Your first antenatal visit: a little preparation can help

Recently found out you are pregnant? Arrange your antenatal consultation early so you can discuss your health, pregnancy history and the care you may need.

Before your appointment, note the date of your last period, make a list of medicines and supplements you take, and gather any previous pregnancy or scan reports. Write down your questions too, including concerns about symptoms, food, work or existing health conditions.
