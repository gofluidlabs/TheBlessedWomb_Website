Anomaly scan in Greater Noida: what does it check?

Your mid-pregnancy ultrasound is an opportunity to understand your baby's development and ask questions about the next steps in your care.

The anomaly scan, also called the Level II scan, examines your baby's developing structures. It also assesses growth and the placenta. It cannot detect every condition, and some views may need to be repeated if your baby's position makes them difficult to obtain.

At The Blessed Womb, Dr. Jyoti Gupta guides clinically indicated pregnancy scans in Alpha I, Greater Noida. Your doctor will advise the appropriate timing for your pregnancy.

Bring your previous scan reports and pregnancy records, and check any preparation instructions with the clinic before your visit.
